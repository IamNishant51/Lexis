# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (v2.1, abridged)

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation in Lexis harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, education, income, nationality,
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards
- Be kind, curious, and precise. Assume good intent; critique code, never people.
- Welcome newcomers; explain acronyms and context.
- Respect scope: small PRs, honest benchmarks, no hype in technical claims.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, unwelcome advances, publishing others'
private information, or any conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
Violations may be reported to the maintainers (open an issue titled `[COC]` or email the
maintainer listed in pyproject). Maintainers will review, warn, or ban as appropriate and
respect reporters' privacy. This Code applies in repo spaces (issues, PRs, discussions).
